# Desert Rogue — Privacy Policy

The privacy policy for **Desert Rogue / طوق الصحراء** on the App Store,
in English and Arabic.

Published at <https://qrom66.github.io/desert-rogue-privacy/>

The app collects nothing: no analytics, no advertising and no accounts. From
version 1.0.3 its only connection is Apple's StoreKit, for optional coin
purchases processed by Apple.
