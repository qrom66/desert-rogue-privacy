Privacy Policy: Desert Rogue

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Desert Rogue does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. None at all.

Who is responsible

Desert Rogue is published by Entilaqco Digital for Website and Portal Design (Commercial Registration No. 255127), which is responsible for this policy. Contact: info@intilaqa.qa

What we collect

Nothing. The iPhone and iPad app contains no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting, no accounts, and no login of any kind. The game itself makes no network requests and sends no data anywhere. The only connection the app makes is Apple's own StoreKit reaching the App Store, from version 1.0.3, to show prices and to process a purchase you choose to make. The store screen also has a Privacy Policy link; if you tap it, your browser opens this page, and the game itself sends nothing.

What is stored on your device

The game saves your progress (coins, gear and its stars, unlocked heroes, talents, settings, high scores, the goals and events you have progressed in, and which purchases and hero trials have been used) to a file inside the app's own storage on your iPhone or iPad. That file never leaves your device. We cannot read it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Third parties

No trackers, no social logins, no advertising and no analytics providers. Purchases are processed by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. From version 1.0.3 the app includes GodotApplePlugins, an open-source bridge that lets the game use Apple's StoreKit; it collects nothing and sends nothing of its own.

Children

The app is safe for all ages and collects nothing from anyone, children included. It is compliant with COPPA by virtue of collecting no data at all. Purchases are optional; if you do not want a child to make them, you can restrict in-app purchases with Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy settings.

Tracking

The app does not track you and does not ask for permission to, because it has nothing to track you with. It never requests access to your camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or location.

Purchases

From version 1.0.3, the app offers optional in-app purchases: coins, packs of coins and gear copies, a daily deal and a weekly bundle, a bundle that is on sale during a calendar event, heroes (each one can be played for one run a day, for seven days, before you decide to buy it), and a subscription, the Caravan Pass, by the month or the year. They are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store: the app never sees your payment details, and it sends nothing about your purchases to us or to anyone else. A record of which purchases have been delivered, and of which deal or bundle you tapped until the App Store has answered, is kept only on your device, in the game's save, so that no purchase is delivered twice. The daily deals, the weekly bundles, the events and the hero trials turn with your device's clock and with a table of dates inside the app; the app contacts no server to learn what is on offer today. For the subscription, the app asks the App Store on your device whether it is active and when the current period ends; that answer stays on your device too. You manage or cancel the subscription in your App Store account settings.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published at this address with a new date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: info@intilaqa.qa
